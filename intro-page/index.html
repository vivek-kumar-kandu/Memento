Medical Advisory Guidelines

The Golden First Aid Protocol

Harmful traditional practices kill more snakebite victims in rural areas than venom alone. Sanjeevini strictly follows National Action Plan for Prevention & Control of Snakebite Envenoming (NAPSE) and WHO protocols.

⚠️

NEVER DELAY MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION

Do not wait for symptoms to appear before seeking care. A harmless-looking bite can cause sudden neurotoxic respiratory failure or hemotoxic bleeding within hours.

✅ WHAT YOU MUST DO
  • 1

    Keep the Patient Completely Still & Calm

    Panic increases heart rate and accelerates systemic venom circulation. Keep the person lying down or comfortably seated.

  • 2

    Immobilize the Bitten Limb

    Splint the bitten extremity loosely using a stick, rolled cloth, or crepe bandage just like a bone fracture. Prevent joint movement.

  • 3

    Remove Rings, Watches & Constrictive Shoes

    Swelling develops rapidly in cytotoxic and viper bites. Constrictive items cause severe ischemia and gangrene if not removed early.

  • 4

    Rush Immediately to a PHC/CHC with ASV

    Call 108 Emergency Ambulance or transport by vehicle. Anti-Snake Venom is the only true therapeutic antidote.

❌ STRICT PROHIBITIONS (DO NOT DO)
  • 1

    DO NOT Cut or Incise the Bite Area

    Slashing the wound severs arteries, causes uncontrollable bleeding in viper bites, and introduces deadly secondary bacterial infections.

  • 2

    DO NOT Attempt to Suck Out Venom

    Suction devices, mouths, or extraction pumps remove negligible venom while inflicting extensive tissue necrosis and mucosal poisoning.

  • 3

    DO NOT Tie Tight Tourniquets or Ropes

    Cutting off arterial blood flow causes severe localized tissue death, limb amputation, and sudden cardiac shock when released.

  • 4

    DO NOT Visit Faith Healers or Apply Herbs

    Every hour spent visiting faith healers (ojhas/tantriks) or applying cow dung and herbal pastes increases mortality exponentially.
