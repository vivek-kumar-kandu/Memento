The Golden First Aid Field Manual

Deadly myths kill more snakebite victims in rural communities than venom itself. Sanjeevini strictly enforces National Action Plan for Prevention & Control of Snakebite Envenoming (NAPSE) guidelines.

⚠️

GOLDEN RULE: NEVER DELAY HOSPITAL TRANSPORTATION

Never wait for symptoms to appear before seeking care. A harmless-looking puncture can trigger acute neurotoxic respiratory paralysis or severe hemotoxic internal hemorrhage within hours.

✅ WHAT YOU MUST DO [LIFE SAVER]
  • 01

    Immobilize the Bitten Limb

    Splint the bitten leg or arm loosely using a stick, rolled cardboard, or cloth just like a bone fracture. Prevent joint flexion.

  • 02

    Keep the Patient Completely Still

    Any muscular exertion or panic accelerates systemic venom lymphatic absorption. Carry the patient; do not let them walk.

  • 03

    Remove Rings, Bangles & Tight Shoes

    Swelling develops rapidly. Metal rings and tight bands cause rapid ischemic necrosis and gangrene if left on.

  • 04

    Rush to a PHC/CHC with Anti-Snake Venom

    Dial 108 Emergency Ambulance immediately. Polyvalent ASV is the only validated clinical antidote.

❌ STRICT PROHIBITIONS [FATAL ERRORS]
  • 01

    DO NOT Cut or Incise the Bite

    Cutting the skin severs arteries, triggers fatal bleeding in viper envenomations, and introduces catastrophic secondary infections.

  • 02

    DO NOT Attempt to Suck Out Venom

    Suction pumps, venom extractors, or oral suction extract zero systemic venom while causing massive local necrosis.

  • 03

    DO NOT Tie Tight Tourniquets or Ropes

    Tourniquets obstruct arterial flow, resulting in limb amputation, gangrene, and sudden lethal venom surge when untied.

  • 04

    DO NOT Visit Faith Healers or Apply Pastes

    Cow dung, herbs, or tantrik rituals waste precious golden hours. Every delayed hour drastically increases fatality.
